About me

From Tabango
to the UC track

I'm Shanily, a 21-year-old from Tabango, Leyte, now in my third year of BS Information Technology at the University of Cebu Main.

My days run on two schedules. On the track I compete as a student-athlete in sprints and jumps. In class I'm still learning the IT side, one project at a time. I'm not a pro at coding yet, and I use AI tools to help me build and understand my work. The track taught me that you get better by showing up and trying again, and I bring that into the classroom too.
